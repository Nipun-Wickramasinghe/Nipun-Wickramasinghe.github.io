01 / ABOUT

About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University at Albany, State University of New York, where I work with Prof. Daphney-Stavroula Zois.

My research explores how machine learning systems can make informed predictions while acquiring only the information needed for a given instance. I am particularly interested in reducing the computational and data requirements of classification through probabilistic modeling, feature acquisition, and classifier assessment.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I completed a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka, and worked in research and development in industry.

Research interests

  • Machine learning
  • Feature acquisition
  • Cost-efficient classification
  • Probabilistic modeling
  • Few-shot learning
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Multi-class & multi-label learning

02 / RESEARCH

Research directions

01

Instance-wise feature acquisition

Developing methods that selectively acquire features for each prediction, balancing classification performance against the cost of obtaining information.

02

Classifier assessment and dependencies

Studying how classifier relationships and probabilistic dependencies can guide efficient decisions about which predictive models to evaluate.

03

Learning with limited supervision

Exploring data-efficient approaches, including few-shot learning, for settings where labeled examples are scarce.

04

Multi-label prediction

Investigating adaptive feature and classifier acquisition for multi-label problems with dependencies among labels.

04 / PROJECTS

Research & projects

A selection of research themes and engineering projects. Repository links can be added as code is made public.

2026 — PRESENT

Instance-wise feature acquisition for multi-label classification

Developing a framework that combines a POMDP-based feature acquisition policy with neural representations to reduce feature acquisition and subproblem assessment costs during multi-label inference.

Current research
2025 — 2026

Instance-wise feature acquisition under few-shot learning

Developed a Siamese-network-based framework to support feature acquisition when only a limited number of labeled samples are available.

Instance-wise feature acquisition for large numbers of classes

Designed a dynamic feature acquisition framework aimed at reducing training time while maintaining classification performance.
